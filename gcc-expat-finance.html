Audience

GCC expat finance, in one wealth OS

Expats and investors across the Gulf often split money across home-country accounts, regional banks, global brokers, and property payment plans. CrossCap is built as a GCC Wealth OS so that complexity stays visible — not scattered across apps and spreadsheets.

Typical challenges

  • Assets denominated in several currencies
  • Investments on more than one exchange
  • Property installments with fixed milestone dates
  • Goals such as liquidity buffers or long-term savings targets
  • No single “home screen” for the full picture

How CrossCap helps

You choose a base currency (including GCC currencies), add holdings and property schedules, and monitor net worth, allocation, and upcoming payments. Optional AI CFO notes summarise concentration and near-term obligations from your data.

The product is GCC-first in workflows and defaults, and useful for global investors who connect to the region — not a single-country-only tool.

What CrossCap is not

Not a bank, not a broker, and not a licensed adviser. It organises and tracks; you remain responsible for decisions and official filings.
