Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

CrossCap (“we”, “our”) is a wealth management dashboard for UAE-based expats and investors. This policy explains what data we process when you use crosscap.app and related services.

1. Who we are

CrossCap is operated as a software product. For privacy questions contact support.crosscap@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data — email and profile details from Google Sign-In (name, email, optional photo).
  • Portfolio data you enter — stock holdings, quantities, cost basis, property details, installments, cash/bank balances, goals, and related notes.
  • Files you upload — e.g. sales/purchase agreements or profile photos, stored in secured cloud storage tied to your account.
  • Technical data — basic logs needed to run authentication and prevent abuse (IP, device/browser type where provided by our providers).

3. How we use data

  • To provide the dashboard (net worth, reminders, goals, AI features you choose to run).
  • To secure accounts and improve reliability.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

We do not sell your personal data. Market prices may be fetched from third-party market data sources; those requests typically use tickers you configured, not your identity.

4. AI features

Optional features (e.g. SPA text/image extraction, AI CFO narrative) may send the content you submit to a third-party AI provider to generate a response. Do not upload documents you are not allowed to process. AI output is for record-keeping assistance only — not legal, tax, or investment advice.

5. Storage & security

Data is stored with industry providers (e.g. Supabase / cloud hosting) with access controls and row-level security so users only access their own records under normal operation. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Sharing

We share data only with infrastructure and auth providers needed to run the service (e.g. Google for login, hosting, database), or when required by law.

7. Retention & deletion

We keep data while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated portfolio data by emailing support. Some logs may be retained for a limited period for security and legal compliance.

8. Your choices

You can update profile fields in Settings, export a backup from Reports (where available), and contact us to correct or delete data. You may disconnect Google access via your Google account permissions.

9. Children

CrossCap is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change; continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy where permitted by law.

11. Contact

support.crosscap@gmail.com
