How to calculate net worth as a UAE expat

Net worth is simply assets minus liabilities. For expats in the UAE, the hard part is not the formula — it is gathering balances across countries and currencies into one clear number.

1. List your assets

  • Bank and cash balances (UAE and abroad)
  • Stocks, ETFs, mutual funds
  • Gold, silver, crypto
  • Property (market estimate or purchase value — be consistent)
  • Receivables, business equity, vehicles if material

2. List your liabilities

  • Mortgages and property loans
  • Personal loans and credit cards
  • Outstanding off-plan installments still owed

3. Convert to one base currency

Pick a base (often AED if you live in the UAE, or your home currency). Convert foreign balances at a recent mid-market rate so the total is comparable month to month.

4. Review monthly, not daily

Markets move daily; decisions improve when you track the trend. Update large items (property, loans) when something material changes.
