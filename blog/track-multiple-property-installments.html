Best way to track multiple property installments

One property is manageable in a spreadsheet. Two or three off-plan plans with different developers quickly becomes a calendar problem.

One row per installment

Each due date should be its own record: property name, stage, amount, status. Sort by date. Mark paid when done.

Prioritise the next payment

Focus on what is due in 30, 14, or 7 days. That is the practical “radar” most investors need.

Connect to cash planning

Installments compete with living costs and investments. Seeing them beside cash and portfolio balances supports better timing decisions.
