How to track Dubai off-plan property payments

Off-plan payments are calendar-driven. Missing a milestone can mean penalties — yet schedules often live only in a developer portal or SPA PDF.

Capture the schedule once

  • Stage name (e.g. booking, construction %)
  • Due date and amount
  • Paid vs scheduled status

Watch the next 30 days

A simple “payment radar” for the next month is more useful than a long static table. Update status when you pay.

Link property to total wealth

Property value and remaining installments both affect net worth. Tracking them next to stocks and cash avoids a false sense of liquidity.
