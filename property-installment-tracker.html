Property installment tracker

Off-plan and staged property payments are easy to miss when they sit outside your brokerage app. CrossCap lets expats and investors across the GCC log property schedules next to stocks, gold, and cash — so wealth and obligations stay in one place.

What you can track

  • Ready or off-plan property records
  • Installment stages, due dates, and amounts
  • Paid, scheduled, or overdue status
  • Updates to dates when plans change
  • Optional email reminders for upcoming due windows (when enabled)

Payment radar

The dashboard highlights installments approaching in the next 30 days so you can plan liquidity before a due date — not after a penalty.

Why this matters for GCC investors

Many investors hold market portfolios and property commitments at the same time. CrossCap’s GCC Wealth OS approach treats installment schedules as first-class data beside liquid assets, instead of leaving them only in developer portals or spreadsheets.

FAQ

Does CrossCap pay installments for me? No. You track and mark status; payments stay with your bank or developer.

Can I use this with investments? Yes. Property sits in the same overall net worth and navigation as portfolio holdings.
