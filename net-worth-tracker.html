Net worth tracker for multi-currency wealth

Net worth is the difference between what you own and what you owe. CrossCap calculates a live consolidated view so expats and investors across the GCC can see the full picture — not just one brokerage or one property.

What CrossCap includes in net worth

  • Stocks & ETFs (multiple markets)
  • Gold & silver holdings
  • Cryptocurrency
  • Cash & bank balances (and card/loan liabilities where logged)
  • Real estate values and related schedules
  • Other assets such as funds, deposits, or vehicles when added

Multi-currency, one base

Holdings can sit in AED, SAR, USD, INR, and other currencies. You choose a base currency (including GCC currencies) so totals convert into one number you can monitor over time.

How to use it

  1. Create an account and select your base currency
  2. Add liquid assets, cash, and property
  3. Review total net worth, allocation, and performance on the home dashboard
  4. Update quantities and mark property installments as paid when needed

FAQ

Is this a bank balance? No. It is your organised estimate of assets minus liabilities based on data you enter and live prices where available.

Does CrossCap give investment advice? No. It is a tracking and organisation tool.
