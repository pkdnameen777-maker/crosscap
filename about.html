About

CrossCap — the GCC Wealth OS for expats & investors

CrossCap is a personal wealth tracking platform built for people who live, work, and invest across the Gulf Cooperation Council and beyond. It brings net worth, global investments, cash, and property payment schedules into one multi-currency dashboard.

What CrossCap is

CrossCap is a wealth operating system for individuals: a place to organise holdings and obligations, not a bank, broker, or licensed financial adviser. You can track:

  • Net worth across assets and liabilities
  • Stocks & ETFs across major markets
  • Gold & silver, crypto, cash & bank accounts
  • Off-plan and ready property with installment schedules
  • Dividends, rental income, and financial goals
  • Optional AI CFO notes to review concentration and upcoming payments

Who it is for

Residents, expats, and investors across the GCC (including the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, and Oman) who hold assets in more than one currency or country and need a single, clear view of wealth and payment milestones.

Positioning

We position CrossCap as the GCC Wealth OS for expats and investors — GCC-first in design and defaults (including multi-currency and property installment workflows), not limited to a single country.

Important disclaimer

CrossCap does not provide investment, tax, or legal advice. AI features help you organise and review your own data. Always use licensed professionals for formal advice.
