Security & data handling

CrossCap stores personal wealth records you enter (holdings, property schedules, goals, and profile details). Below is an honest summary of how that works today — not marketing claims we cannot verify.

Authentication

Sign-in uses Google OAuth via Supabase Auth. We do not store your Google password. Sessions are managed by the authentication provider.

Where data is stored

Application data is stored in a managed cloud database (Supabase / PostgreSQL). File uploads such as property documents, when enabled, are stored in secured object storage tied to your account.

Bank connections

CrossCap does not connect directly to your bank login today. Cash and balances are entered by you. We do not request or store bank passwords.

Market prices

Live prices, when available, come from third-party market data providers. Prices can be delayed or unavailable for some symbols.

Documents (e.g. SPA uploads)

If you upload a sales or purchase agreement for AI extraction, the file is processed to suggest fields you can review before saving. Always verify extracted data. Do not upload documents you are not allowed to process.

AI features

AI CFO notes and extraction helpers use your portfolio context to generate organisational insights. They are not licensed financial, tax, or legal advice. Provider processing follows each vendor’s terms.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your portfolio data as a product listing
  • We do not claim “bank-grade” or certified compliance we have not obtained
  • We do not replace official Golden Visa or government eligibility decisions

Your controls

You can update profile details, export backups from Reports (when available), and request account or data deletion by emailing support@crosscap.app.
