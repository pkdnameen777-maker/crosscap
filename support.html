Common topics

Google login / can’t reach dashboard +

Use the same Google account every time. Allow pop-ups for login. If the page is blank after login, hard-refresh (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+R) or try an incognito window.

Duplicate properties or holdings +

The app auto-cleans many duplicates on login. Avoid restoring the same backup twice. If copies remain, email support with your login email.

Net worth looks too low +

Total Net Worth should include stocks, gold & silver, crypto, real estate, bank balances, and receivables, minus cards and debts. Confirm property prices and cash entries are saved under your account.

Live prices missing +

Some tickers or markets may not resolve. Your cost basis still shows. Check market selection (e.g. UAE vs India) matches the exchange you trade on.
