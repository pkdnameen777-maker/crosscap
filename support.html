Common questions

How do I change my base currency? +

Open Settings → Preferences → Base currency. GCC currencies (AED, SAR, QAR, KWD, BHD, OMR) are listed first. Choose your currency and click Save profile. Consolidated totals (net worth, portfolio, cash, other assets, property cards) convert via live FX. Golden Visa progress stays in AED because the UAE threshold is defined in dirhams.

What does total net worth include? +

Net worth = liquid holdings (stocks & ETFs, gold & silver, crypto) + real estate + other assets (mutual funds, FDs, bonds/sukuk, vehicles, business) + bank & receivables − credit cards − other debts. AI CFO evaluates this full picture, not only stocks and gold.

Which markets and currencies are supported? +

GCC markets are listed first when adding assets (UAE, Saudi, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain), then South Asia, developed markets, Asia, and alternatives (crypto, gold, silver). Cash and other assets support a wide set of currencies with GCC options first.

Why does Google sign-in mention supabase.co? +

Auth is powered by Supabase behind auth.crosscap.app. Google’s consent screen may still show the project domain during transition. Ensure Google OAuth origins/redirects and Supabase Site URL / Redirect URLs include your CrossCap domains. Contact support if login fails after setup.

I see duplicate holdings or properties +

The app auto-cleans many duplicates on login. Avoid restoring the same backup twice. If copies remain, email support with your login email.

Net worth looks too low or wrong currency +

Confirm every sleeve is saved: Portfolio, Real Estate, Cash & Bank, Other assets. Then check Settings → Base currency and save again so totals refresh. Property contract values are stored in AED and converted to your base currency for display.

Live prices missing +

Some tickers or markets may not resolve. Your cost basis still shows. Check market selection (e.g. UAE vs India, or crypto) matches the exchange you trade on.

What does AI CFO analyse? +

AI CFO builds a full net-worth snapshot: liquid holdings by market and type, real estate, other assets, cash, liabilities, installment schedule, goals, and UAE Golden Visa property progress. The narrative and chat use that entire picture. It is organisational guidance only — not regulated investment advice.

Golden Visa progress seems stuck +

Only UAE real estate contract values count toward the AED 2M indicator. Stocks, overseas property, and other assets are excluded by design. Add or update UAE properties under Real Estate.
