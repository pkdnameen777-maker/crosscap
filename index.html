Core Capabilities

All Your Global Assets, One AED Currency

No more switching between brokerage apps, physical spreadsheets, and bank portals. CrossCap consolidates your DFM, US, and Indian stock portfolios alongside physical gold, silver, and liquid cash into a unified AED net-worth dashboard.

Never Miss a Dubai Real Estate Installment

Managing off-plan property payment plans across multiple developers can lead to missed milestones and overdue penalties. CrossCap tracks every installment date, calculates your remaining equity, and sends automated WhatsApp and email reminders before every due date.

Real-Time Dividend & Rental Yields

Understand your true monthly cash flow. CrossCap tracks incoming stock dividends across global exchanges alongside net property rental yields, giving you a clear forecast of your financial runway in the UAE.
